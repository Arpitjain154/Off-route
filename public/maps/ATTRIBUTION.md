The unmodified OSM XML is bundled at android/app/src/main/assets/maps/bhopal.osm.
The display tiles derive from that extract using scripts/prepare-map.py and scripts/build-tiles.mjs.
The route.json polyline is a real OSM road used for the explicitly labeled simulated drive.

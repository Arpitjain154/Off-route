# Android 1.2: GPS, stationary speed and Madhya Pradesh

The old engine required a moving-course alignment before zero-velocity updates, preventing stop detection on startup. It only used GPS velocity at the initial heading alignment. Inertial integration could therefore show speed while a phone was stationary. The update permits GNSS-confirmed stops before heading alignment, applies continuing reliable GPS velocity measurements, and uses fresh measured GPS speed for the speed display. Stops require both quiet IMU and recent low-speed GNSS, rather than assuming low IMU variance means a stopped car. Motion or confirmed GPS movement releases the stop.

Initial tilt now waits for one second of actual phone gravity samples. GPS rejects stale, out-of-order, poor-accuracy or nonfinite fixes. Missing raw C/N0 callbacks no longer suppress a valid position fix. GPS updates use horizontal position rather than treating uncertain altitude as precise. Three consistent, accurate rejected fixes can increase position covariance to recover from accumulated drift through the existing filter. Map corrections cannot move a confirmed stationary phone or override fresh accurate GPS. The first real map position does not animate from a fictitious Bhopal starting location.

Settings exposes GPS fix age/accuracy and the speed source. A confirmed stop displays zero and is excluded from drive-distance accumulation. During a signal gap, moving speed remains an inertial estimate and can drift; this update does not claim accurate speed without a reference under every condition.

The map source is the 2026-09-11 Geofabrik Central Zone extract (351,181,859 bytes), verified against its published MD5. Madhya Pradesh's OSM administrative relation 1950071 supplies the state boundary. The build creates statewide roads/water/parks tiles and an on-device GraphHopper road graph. Central Bhopal retains its detailed building overlay. Statewide tiles are generated through zoom 13 and overzoomed at street scale; they are not a statewide building/POI inventory. OSM data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0.

Reproduce with `scripts/download-mp-map.ps1`, then `scripts/build-mp-map.ps1` (Python 3.12 with osmium 4.3.1 and shapely 2.1.2, Node and JDK 21). Downloads and intermediate geometry are in `data/maps`; packaged assets are local and require no map API key. A state-sized map makes the APK larger than the original Bhopal-only build.

IO-VNBD models remain trained and versioned as 1.0.0, with learned fusion corrections disabled pending device validation. Model evaluation is in `ml/MODEL-CARD.md`. No Android device was connected via ADB during this update, so physical-phone verification remains required. Install the rebuilt APK over the existing app, start outdoors, wait for a fresh fix, remain still for several seconds, then walk or drive safely to check that speed leaves zero and follows GPS. Review Settings diagnostics if the phone reports no reliable speed measurement.
